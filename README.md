# Manual Patient Bed for Home Care

A manual patient bed can support comfortable positioning and everyday caregiving for patients who need assistance at home. It may be suitable for elderly individuals, people with limited mobility, and patients recovering from illness or surgery, depending on their care needs.

## Benefits of a Manual Patient Bed

- Supports patient positioning and comfort.
- Helps caregivers with routine bedside care.
- Can be useful in home healthcare settings.
- Provides a practical option for patients who require extended bed rest.

## Choosing Medical Equipment for Home

Before choosing a patient bed, consider the patient's mobility, positioning needs, caregiver requirements, and available space.
